Account & Data Deletion Request

Community Calendar App

Important: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account information (email, username)
  • Profile data (zip code, preferences)
  • Events you've created
  • Your RSVPs to events
  • Your favorited events
  • All associated account data

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, please send an email to:

Request Account Deletion

Or manually email: dylanmayne@gmail.com

Required Information

Please include the following in your deletion request:

  • Email address associated with your account
  • Username (if you remember it)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days of receiving your email. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been permanently deleted.

Alternative: Delete Within the App

You can also delete your account directly from the Community Calendar app:

  1. Open the Community Calendar app
  2. Go to Settings or Profile
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion

Note: This feature may be added in a future update.

Questions?

If you have any questions about the deletion process or our data practices, please review our Privacy Policy or contact us at dylanmayne@gmail.com.
